02 — La Nostra Storia

A trattoria, not a restaurant

“A table is a promise: whoever sits down is family until they leave.”

— attributed to the fictional founder, Elide Palazzo, 1962

Fourteen tables, one copper pot

The story we invented for this concept goes like this: Elide Palazzo opened with fourteen tables and her mother's copper ruoto, the pot that never leaves the flame. Ragù goes in at dawn and comes out when it is ready, which is never before six.

Nothing is rushed and little is written down. The menu changes when the vegetables change, the wine is local because walking far is difficult, and the bread arrives before you ask because that is what arrives first.

Why this page exists

Palazzo is not a real place — there is no kitchen, no nonna, no waitlist on Fridays. It is a design exercise: a one-page brand microsite built in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript to explore warm palettes, kinetic type, and hand-feel interactions without a framework in sight.

Every word, dish and price on this page was written as sample content for the concept. If it reads like a real trattoria, the craft did its job.
